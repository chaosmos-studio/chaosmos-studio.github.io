    A calm arrow puzzle

    Arrows rest on orbits around a warm sun. Tap one and it flies straight the way it points: out of the system if its path is clear, or back with a bump if another arrow is in the way. Read the paths, then clear the sky.

    • Free to play
    • No timers
    • Plays offline
    • Never an ad mid-level

    How to play

    Look. Tap. Clear.

    1. 1Look

      Hold an arrow to see its path, a band exactly as wide as the arrow. If another arrow touches the band, it is in the way.

    2. 2Tap

      Tap an arrow whose path is clear and it flies straight out of the system, ringing a note as it goes.

    3. 3Clear

      Every arrow that leaves opens the way for others. Launch the last one out, without a miss, for three stars.

    What makes it special

    One rule, read at a glance

    • One tap, one straight line

      Tap an arrow and it flies the way it points. If nothing is in its path, it leaves the system for good.

    • Straight paths, round orbits

      The arrows rest on curved orbits but fly in straight lines, so who is in whose way has to be read, not counted along a row.

    • Three hearts, three stars

      A blocked arrow bounces back and costs a heart. Clear a level without a single miss for three stars.

    • Hold to see the way out

      Keep a finger on an arrow to lift it and see its path, drawn as wide as the arrow. Slide onto a neighbour to switch before anything counts.

    • A hint you can trust

      The hint lights an arrow that is free right now, the one whose launch frees the most others, and draws its way out.

    • A new rule every chapter

      Each chapter of 20 levels brings its own idea to the sky: locks, ghosts, two suns, wormholes, mirrors, prisms and more.

    About the game

    Behind Apsis

    How it is made

    Every level is built backwards. A program adds arrows one at a time, and only ever places an arrow whose own path is clear of everything already there, so taking them away newest first always works. A solver then measures how hard each level is, the levels are ordered so none is easier than the one before, and no two are near-copies of each other, even turned or mirrored. The tests check all of that again on every build.

    A flight is exact geometry, not a physics simulation, so what you see, what the solver computes and what the generator builds always agree. The art, the sounds and the music are made in code, in GDScript on the Godot Engine.

    How it stays free

    Apsis is free to play, with no account, no timers and no energy to wait for.

    It has a few ads, placed so they never touch a puzzle: a banner below the sky and on the level list, an ad between levels now and then, and short videos you can choose to watch, for a hint or to try a pack. Its shop is optional, since every level opens by playing. The Supporter pack turns the ads off for good and makes every hint free, and the other packs, level keys and the tip jar help pay for the next game.

    Questions

    Frequently asked

    More answers, and a place to report a bug
    How do I play Apsis?

    Tap an arrow and it flies straight the way it points. If nothing is in its path, it leaves the system. If another arrow is in the way, it hits, springs back and costs one of your three hearts. Launch every arrow out to clear the level.

    Is Apsis free?

    Yes. Apsis is a free Android game, coming soon to Google Play. It shows a few ads, never during a level, and its shop is optional: every level opens by playing.

    Does Apsis need an internet connection?

    No. Apsis plays completely offline, and your progress is saved on your device. The Android app goes online only for its ads and for purchases through Google Play. Without a connection no ads appear, and a hint that would ask for a video is given anyway.

    How do I get three stars?

    Your stars are the hearts you have left when the last arrow leaves: three stars for no misses, two for one miss, one for two. Some later levels need a miss on purpose, to break a brittle arrow, and that miss costs no star. Lose all three hearts and you can retry, or take one more heart once a try, which earns one star if you clear the sky with it.

    Ready to launch?

    Apsis is free, with nothing to sign up for. It is coming soon to Google Play.
