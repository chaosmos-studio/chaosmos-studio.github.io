How to play

Look. Tilt. Drop.

1

Look

Every marble sits on a paper board with walls, holes and, later, pits. Find where each one needs to go.

2

Tilt

Swipe to tilt the board. Every marble rolls at once, until a wall, the edge or another marble stops it.

3

Drop

A marble that rolls over a hole of its own colour drops in. Get them all home in as few moves as you can.

Why it's good

Simple rules, surprisingly deep

One swipe moves everything

Tilt the board and every marble rolls at once, until a wall, the edge or another marble stops it.

Each colour has its home

A marble drops into a hole of its own colour the moment it rolls over one. Plan the route before you tip.

Watch out for pits

Square pits swallow any marble. Undo is always one tap away, so every mistake is a lesson, not a loss.

A hint that shows, not tells

Stuck? The hint previews your next tilt on the board: ghost marbles, dotted trails and where each one lands.

Stars for the perfect route

Every level is solved by a computer to find the fewest moves. Match that par for three stars.

Calm by design

No timers, no lives, no energy. Play offline, at your own pace, with sound, music and vibration you control.

Questions

Frequently asked

How do I play Plumb?

Swipe up, down, left or right to tilt the board. Every marble rolls in that direction until something stops it. A marble that rolls over a hole of its own colour drops in. Get every marble home to clear the level.

Is Plumb free?

Yes. Plumb is free on Google Play, and you can also play it in your web browser. The Android app has a few ads, never during play, and a one-time Supporter pack that turns them off.

Does Plumb need an internet connection?

No. Plumb plays completely offline, and your progress is saved on your device. The Android app goes online only for its ads and the Supporter pack, so without a connection the ads simply do not appear.

What does par mean, and how do I get three stars?

Par is the fewest moves that clear a level, found by a solver that checks every possible move. Clear a level in par for three stars; a few moves over still earns one or two.
