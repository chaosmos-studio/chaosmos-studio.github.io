## Full description (1484 / 4000)

Seiche is a calm puzzle about a pond full of fish. A seiche is a slow wave that rocks a whole lake from shore to shore, and here every tap sends one across the water.

Tap the water and every fish in line with the ripple swims straight away from it. A fish keeps swimming until the bank, a rock or another fish stops it, or until it swims into a net. Get every fish into the nets. One tap can send fish two ways at once, or push a whole line of them together, so the trick is choosing where the ripple starts.

- One rule you learn in a few taps, and ponds that keep getting cleverer
- Three stars for catching every fish in the fewest taps
- Undo any tap and restart any time. No timers on the ponds, no lives, no energy
- Taps that move many fish at once ring out, and every catch pops its net
- A new place at every pond: a glass bowl, a koi garden in the rain, a frozen lake, a rooftop pool, a starlit lake, each with its own water, fish and weather, through a whole day from dawn to night
- Dozens of looks to collect for your fish, ripples and nets, from rubber ducks to neon tetras
- A hint that shows where to tap next
- A daily pond, the same for everyone, with a clock, a streak to keep and a calendar of past days
- An album of eight kinds of fish to fill, some of them rare, each with its biggest catch
- Every clear makes a card to share or save
- Colour themes and pond looks, each in light and dark
- Gentle water sounds and vibration you can switch off
- Plays offline
