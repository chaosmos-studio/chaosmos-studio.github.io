## Full description (1433 / 4000)

Drag the knots until no line crosses another. A calm untangle puzzle and brain teaser, where a cleared web often settles into a real constellation.

Every level is a web of knots and strands, and some strands cross. Drag a round knot anywhere and let go. When no strand crosses another and no knot sits on a strand, the web is clear. The name comes from the sky too: Pyxis, the little compass in the southern sky.

Square knots never move, so you work around them. Some knots slide along a rail, others follow a circle, and every chapter changes how a pull unfolds.

- Every web built from a solution, each a little harder than the last
- Real constellations to untangle on the night sky, from little Aries to Orion the hunter
- Three stars for clearing a web within its goal, with a hint that rings the knot to move and circles where it goes
- Chapters that each add a twist: anchors, rails, rings and webs that keep growing
- A daily web, the same for everyone, with a clock, a streak to keep and a calendar of past days
- Sign in with Play Games for achievements, leaderboards and progress that follows you to a new phone
- Every clear makes a card to share or save
- Colour themes and new ways to draw the web, each in light and dark
- Undo and restart any time. No timers on the levels, no lives, no energy
- Original music whose melody rises as the web clears, with sound, music and vibration you can switch off
- Plays offline
