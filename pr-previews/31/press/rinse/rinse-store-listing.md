## Full description (1115 / 4000)

Rinse is a calm puzzle about washing things clean.

Tap above the dirt to pour. The water runs down, lifts the grime off every tile it touches and carries it away. But dirty water drops its dirt again wherever it slows down, so the order you wash in decides whether the water lasts.

The first chapters bring one new piece each, taught as you play: drains, mud that one splash knocks loose, baths that hold whatever reaches them, plugs that let a bath drain, and trays that tip their water out once they are full. After that, every place mixes them in new ways, and every level is a new thing to wash, from a snowman to a lighthouse and a rocket.

- Levels that grow steadily harder, every one with a solution
- Collect tile sets, waters and grimes as you play, and wear any mix
- Three stars for washing a level in its goal, with a hint when you are stuck
- Undo any pour, or start again
- A daily puzzle, the same for everyone, with a streak to keep
- Every win makes a card to share or save
- Tile sets and colour themes, each in light and dark
- No timers. Sound and vibration you can switch off
- Plays offline
